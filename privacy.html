Privacy & Terms

WHAT WE STORE

Neon Drift Arena runs entirely in your browser. The only data it keeps is stored locally on your device, never sent to us: your control bindings and settings, whether you've seen the first-time tutorial, and whether you've seen the midgame ad once. We collect no personal data and run no analytics of our own.

CRAZYGAMES

When played on CrazyGames, the CrazyGames SDK handles sign-in, ads, and multiplayer invites under its own privacy policy and terms.
